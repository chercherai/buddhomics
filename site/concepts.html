The 5,000 most frequent Pali terms, placed by the company they keep across the canon (LSA term vectors, t-SNE). The classic numbered lists — aggregates, dependent origination, wings to awakening, divine abidings — are colored so you can see whether the doctrine's own groupings live together in usage-space. Zoom in for labels.

Term embeddings are rows of V·S from the same 100-dim SVD behind the document map, so a term sits near the documents — and other terms — it co-occurs with. Size tracks how many texts a term appears in. Stems are matched by prefix, so inflected forms of a list term inherit its color.
