Every text of the Pali canon, placed by what it talks about. TF-IDF over the Pali → SVD → UMAP. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, hover for a text's strongest terms. Click a legend key to hide a collection.

Substrate: bilara-data (Mahāsaṅgīti Pali). 6,959 texts with ≥200 characters of Pali. Layout is UMAP over 100-dim SVD of a 40k-term TF-IDF matrix; proximity means lexical similarity, and cluster shapes are suggestive, not proof.
